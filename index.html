Insights
Log a few sessions to unlock personalized insights about your volume, intensity, and recovery balance.
Monday
Lift + Z2

Heavy upper push/pull. Bench, weighted pull-ups, OHP, rows, biceps and triceps accessories. Evening: 45–60 min Zone 2 bike or easy run.

Tuesday
Hyrox mixed

4–6 rounds: 800m run + 1 station (rotate wall balls, burpee broad jumps, sled push, ski erg, row). Race-pace simulation of compromised running.

Wednesday
Lower lift

Squat or deadlift focus. Lunges, RDLs or hip thrusts, calves, core. Optional 30 min Zone 2 walk or bike after.

Thursday
Long Zone 2

60–90 min steady aerobic work. Run or bike, conversational pace. Talk test: full sentences with effort. The big aerobic stimulus of the week.

Friday
Upper lift

Different exercise selection from Monday. Incline pressing, chin-ups, lateral and rear delts, direct arm work. Higher rep ranges (8–15) for hypertrophy.

Saturday
Strength endurance

Implement grind. Sled push intervals, sandbag lunges for distance, farmers carries, wall ball volume. Heavier and slower than Tuesday.

Sunday
Rest

Full rest, or 30–45 min very easy walk or bike if you feel like moving. No structured work.

Base building
May – June

Build aerobic engine. Maximize Zone 2 volume (3–5+ hrs/wk), establish lifting baseline, get comfortable with HYROX implements. 80/20 intensity split. Track HRV trend.

Build phase
July – August

Layer in race-specific intensity. Tuesday sessions get sharper. Add tempo and threshold work to long runs. Lifting volume holds; intensity stays high. Deload every 4th week.

Race specificity
September

HYROX simulations in full. Practice exact transitions, pacing strategy with your partner. Lifting drops slightly to make room for race-pace work. This is when fitness sharpens.

Taper
Final 7–10 days

Volume drops 40–60%. Intensity stays (short, sharp). Sleep maxes out, nutrition dialed. Do not test fitness in taper week. Trust the work.
